Revenue Recognition Policy - PT Arunika Cloud Commerce
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Owner: Finance Controller

Policy summary:
1. SaaS subscription revenue is recognized over time when platform access is made available to the customer.
2. Implementation revenue is recognized when the relevant milestone is accepted by the customer or when production readiness is formally confirmed.
3. Contracts above IDR 750,000,000 require Commercial Committee approval before revenue can be recognized.
4. Manual revenue journal entries above IDR 50,000,000 require CFO approval.
5. Invoices issued in the last five business days of a reporting period must be supported by acceptance evidence, usage report, or delivery log.
6. Company-prepared reports are not independent source evidence. They must be paired with contracts, invoices, bank evidence, and reviewer attestations.
